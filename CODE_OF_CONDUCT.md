# Code of Conduct

ClosedSesame welcomes contributors who are curious, careful, and kind.
Participants must help keep discussions and contributions respectful and free
from harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, and deliberate
exposure of private information.

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when conduct harms the
community or puts users at risk. Report a serious conduct concern privately to
the maintainer through GitHub; do not publish sensitive personal details in an
issue.

By participating, you agree to act in good faith, explain technical decisions
clearly, and make room for people with different backgrounds and experience.
